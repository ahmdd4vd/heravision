# Expanded fixture visual observations

The expanded contact sheet contains the original 22 reviewed samples plus eight screenshot/document UI assets and four ambiguous candidates.

The eight added screenshot samples are visually consistent with `screenshot_document`: dashboards, cloud interfaces, Word/table interfaces, moderation tables, and desktop application windows. They are answerable at the domain level, although source/license review is still pending.

The four added ambiguous candidates are mixed in quality: a blank scan is non-informative, while three motion-blur/street images visibly contain scenes but are difficult for precise object recognition. They are suitable as provisional `ambiguous`/abstain candidates, but source review is pending and they must not be considered independent benchmark ground truth.

The original 22 samples remain 13 natural photos and 9 diagrams/documents. The expanded candidate therefore has 34 samples: 13 natural_photo, 9 diagram_document, 8 screenshot_document, and 4 ambiguous.
